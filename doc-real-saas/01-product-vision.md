# Product Vision

## One-sentence concept

Let a 2–20 person business use credits to create and manage an attendance company where every member uses a personal Google account and every attendance device is explicitly approved by the company.

## Problem

Very small companies often use paper, chat messages, or spreadsheets for attendance. These methods are easy to start but make corrections, monthly summaries, accountability, and payroll preparation difficult. Larger HR platforms can be too expensive and complex for this segment.

## Primary customer

**Proposed:** an owner, office manager, or operations lead in a company with 2–20 workers who needs attendance records but does not need a complete HR suite.

Potential early segments to validate:

- Small offices and agencies
- Retail shops and small service businesses
- Clinics, studios, workshops, and local teams
- Small remote or hybrid teams

These are hypotheses, not confirmed markets. The first segment should be chosen through interviews.

## Users

- **Owner:** obtains or receives credits, uses them to create and manage the company, invites people, assigns permissions, reviews attendance, and controls billing.
- **Authorized admin/manager:** can perform specific delegated actions, including approving a member’s device with a PTP.
- **Employee/member:** signs in with a personal Google account, joins invited companies, registers each device, clocks in/out, and sees personal records.

## Core value proposition

- Setup in minutes rather than days
- One personal identity that can access one or more invited companies
- Company-controlled approval of every device used for attendance
- A daily action that takes only a few seconds
- Clear records for both worker and owner
- Simple monthly review and export
- Predictable, small-business-friendly cost
- Flexible credits that can support subscriptions, fixed purchases, trials, and future paid capabilities

## Product principles

1. **Small-team first.** Optimize every screen and workflow for 2–20 people.
2. **Trust through transparency.** Employees can see their own records and every correction.
3. **Simple defaults, explicit policy.** Avoid enterprise configuration, but make time zone, workweek, and correction rules clear.
4. **Company-controlled devices.** Joining a company does not automatically authorize every device for attendance.
5. **Personal identity, company permission.** Google identifies the person; the company controls membership and attendance-device access.
6. **Exportable by design.** A customer can retrieve their data and leave without friction.

## Positioning

“Create your company, invite your team, approve their devices, and control attendance from one simple place.”

## Business objective

Earn revenue when owners obtain credits and use them for company creation, company management, and other paid capabilities. Credits let the business offer subscriptions, one-time fixed purchases, trials, bundles, promotions, or future charging models without changing the product’s common commercial language. Invited members do not pay merely to join.

## Boundaries

The first product is attendance software, not payroll, surveillance, access control, workforce scheduling, or a full human-resource information system.
